Step 1: Make your key

Every message is locked so that only you can open it. Not the website host, not the developer, nobody. Your key is made right here in your browser and protected by a passphrase only you know.

At least 10 characters. A short sentence is easy to remember and hard to guess. This is different from your login password.

Step 2: Save your backup key

If you ever forget your passphrase, this backup key is the only way back in. Write it down or print it, and keep it somewhere locked. Anyone who has it can read your messages.
